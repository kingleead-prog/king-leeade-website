ABOUT KING LEEADE

I am KING LEEADE, a professional guitarist with a deep passion for gospel music and ministry. I serve as a lead and rhythm guitarist, committed to excellence, spirit-filled expression, and creating an atmosphere where heaven meets earth. Through my music, I seek to use every note as a vessel for worship, encouragement, and ministry, bringing expressive tone, musical sensitivity, and excellence to every assignment.

From the stage to the studio, he serves with a heart for God and a commitment to quality sound. His musical workflow includes guitars, effects processors, FL Studio, Behringer UMC22 and his Epiphone Les Paul.

“Music is my ministry, and my guitar is my voice.”
