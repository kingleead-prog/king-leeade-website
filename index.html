About King LeeaDe

King LeeaDe is a professional gospel guitarist, minstrel and music producer specializing in lead and rhythm guitar. His passion is to use music as an instrument of worship, inspiration and ministry.

Through expressive guitar playing, live performance and professional music production, he brings a distinctive sound to worship gatherings, recordings, rehearsals and live events.

His musical workflow includes guitars, effects processors, FL Studio, Behringer UMC22 and his Epiphone Les Paul.
